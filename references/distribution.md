# Formats, variants, and delivery

Read this when a master needs platform variants, social cuts, a pitch-deck embed, or a reusable launch package.

## Master first

Finish one canonical high-resolution master before deriving variants. Preserve sufficient source resolution and wider framing so vertical and square crops remain possible. Do not independently rebuild the story for each format until the master narrative is accepted.

## Format intent

| Format | Common use | Composition note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16:9 | website, YouTube, pitch deck | best for dashboard context and side-by-side proof |
| 16:10 | desktop-product showcase | preserves more application height |
| 9:16 | Reels, Shorts, TikTok | one focal subject; recompose rather than center-crop blindly |
| 1:1 | feeds and embeds | keep text and UI inside a central safe region |
| 4:5 | social feed | useful compromise for product plus copy |

For vertical output, redesign dense UI moments: crop to one panel, stack proof, or use a clean picture-in-picture layout. Never shrink an entire desktop dashboard until it becomes unreadable.

## Variant strategy

Launch and pitch work benefits from inexpensive copy variants. Preserve the accepted body and vary only the hook, first-frame copy, or CTA. Keep a small variant matrix with intended audience, promise, proof, opening frame, CTA, output path, and review status.

Do not generate variants that change factual claims without updating the claim ledger and source.

## Delivery package

A professional package may contain:

- canonical master MP4;
- platform variants with explicit dimensions;
- poster frame or thumbnail;
- captions or transcript when narration exists;
- production plan, capture plan, finish plan, and timeline source;
- raw captures and licensed source assets when requested;
- asset and music provenance;
- technical validation output, contact sheet, checksum, and known limitations.

Name outputs by product, purpose, aspect ratio, resolution, version, and status. Avoid ambiguous names such as `final-final-2.mp4`.
